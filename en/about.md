# About Bero

Canonical page: [https://bero.land/en/about](https://bero.land/en/about)

Roberto Junior, known as Bero, is a software engineer, creator and illustrator based in Sao Jose dos Campos, Brazil. He has been programming since 2016 and working professionally with software for more than six years.

He currently works as a Software Engineer at Ask.com, focusing on Golang and Python. His experience also includes Node.js, TypeScript, React, Next.js and TanStack.

Bero started with Portugol in VisualG, moved on to C and Java, built games with Construct 2 and later fell in love with web development and the ability to create his own apps.

He also publishes short animations about everyday life as a developer, father and content creator.
