# About Bero

Canonical page: [https://bero.land/en/about](https://bero.land/en/about)

Hey! Roberto Junior, better known online as Bero, is a software engineer, content creator and illustrator based in Sao Jose dos Campos, Brazil. He has been programming since 2016 and working professionally with software for more than six years.

He currently works as a Software Engineer at Ask.com, focusing on Golang and Python. His experience also includes Node.js, TypeScript, React, Next.js and TanStack.

Bero started his career as a programmer in 2016, when he enrolled in the Computer Science program at UFG. He wrote his first lines of code in Portugol, C and Java, created his first game with Construct 2 and, in 2020, fell in love with full-stack web development and the ability to build his own apps from end to end.

In 2022, he returned to content creation, focusing primarily on helping beginner developers. These days, he tries to balance his life as an international developer, content creator, indie hacker and father.
